# Kiểm tra TikZ

## Hình học cơ bản

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \draw[thick,->] (-0.5,0) -- (4,0) node[right] {x};
  \draw[thick,->] (0,-0.5) -- (0,3) node[above] {y};
  \draw[fill=blue!20] (0,0) rectangle (2,1.5);
  \draw[red,thick] (0,0) -- (3,2.5);
  \node[circle,fill=black,inner sep=1.5pt] at (3,2.5) {};
  \node at (3.3,2.5) {A};
\end{tikzpicture}
```

## Đồ thị hàm số (pgfplots)

```tikz
\begin{axis}[width=9cm,height=6cm,grid=both,xlabel=x,ylabel=y,
             domain=-3:3,samples=100,legend pos=north west]
  \addplot[blue,thick]{x^2};
  \addplot[red,thick]{2*x+1};
  \legend{$x^2$,$2x+1$}
\end{axis}
```
